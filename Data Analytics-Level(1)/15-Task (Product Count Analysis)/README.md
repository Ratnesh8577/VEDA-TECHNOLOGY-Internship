# Superstore Sales Project – Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based Superstore Sales Analysis** designed to analyze sales and profitability across different regions, provinces, customer segments, and product sub-categories.

The main objective is to identify:

* Which product sub-categories generate the highest profits
* Which product sub-categories generate the highest losses
* Which regions and provinces perform better or worse
* Where loss-making products may require business attention
* Regional patterns and possible business anomalies

The project uses **Microsoft Excel**, including filtering, sorting, conditional formatting, and Pivot Tables.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Imagine you are a sales manager at **Superstore Sales**, one of Canada's largest retail stores.

The company has sales data covering different regions of the country. The goal is to analyze the data and determine which product sub-categories are generating profits and which are causing losses.

These insights can help management consider strategies such as:

* Reviewing loss-making product sub-categories
* Improving pricing or sales strategies
* Understanding regional performance
* Identifying products that require further investigation
* Supporting data-driven business decisions

# 📌 Project Activities

## Activity 1 – Corporate Customer Segment Analysis

The first activity focuses on the **Corporate customer segment**.

### 1. Basic Formatting

The Excel dataset was formatted to improve readability.

Tasks include:

* Adjusting column widths
* Formatting the header row
* Applying suitable header colors
* Adding borders to header cells

---

### 2. Customer Segment Filtering

The raw dataset was filtered according to customer segment.

Separate worksheets were created for the available customer segments.

For **Activity 1**, the analysis focuses specifically on the:

> **Corporate Customer Segment**

---

### 3. Report Preparation

The report was prepared by:

* Freezing the header row
* Removing or hiding unnecessary columns
* Keeping relevant fields required for sales and profit analysis

---

### 4. Sorting

The Corporate data was organized using multiple levels of sorting:

1. Sort by **Region** alphabetically
2. Within each Region, sort by **Province** alphabetically
3. Within each Province, sort by **Sales** in descending order

This makes regional and provincial sales comparisons easier.

---

## 🎨 Conditional Formatting

### Top 10% Sales Orders

Within each Region and Province, the top **10% of orders by Sales** are highlighted using:

* Light green fill
* Dark green border

This helps identify high-value sales orders quickly.

### Profit and Loss Formatting

Profitability is represented using a color scale:

* Higher profits → darker green
* Lower profits/losses → darker red

This provides a quick visual understanding of profitable and loss-making transactions.

---

## 💵 Number and Date Formatting

The following formatting was applied:

* Sales rounded to **one decimal place**
* Profit rounded to **one decimal place**
* Sales displayed in **US dollar ($) format**
* Profit displayed in **US dollar ($) format**
* Order Date formatted into a readable date format
* Ship Date formatted into a readable date format

Example:

`15-Jan-2012`

---

## 📑 Regional Report Formatting

Double bottom borders are applied to separate different Regions.

This improves the readability of the final report and clearly separates regional sections.

# 📈 Activity 2 – Profitability Analysis

Activity 2 focuses on identifying profitable and loss-making product sub-categories across regions.

---

## 🏆 1. High-Profit Categories

Pivot Tables are used to identify the:

> **Top 3 most profitable Product Sub-Categories in each Region**

The analysis helps determine:

* Which sub-categories generate the highest profits
* Which products perform consistently across regions
* Which regions contribute strongly to product profitability
* Whether certain sub-categories show unusual regional performance

Additional comparisons can be performed using conditional formatting and profit-based sorting.

---

## 📉 2. Loss-Making Categories

The analysis also identifies the most loss-making product sub-categories.

The process includes:

1. Finding the most loss-making Product Sub-Categories across all regions
2. Identifying the regions where those sub-categories perform poorly
3. Examining unusual or unexpected patterns
4. Considering possible business explanations for the observed results

Special attention is given to the relationship between:

* Tables
* Chairs
* Regional profitability
* Customer purchasing behavior
