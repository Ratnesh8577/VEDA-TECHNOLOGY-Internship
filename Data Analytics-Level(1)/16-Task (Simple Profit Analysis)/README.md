# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard — Excel

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Excel dashboard** designed to analyze retail sales, profit, discounts, customer segments, categories, and regional performance.

The dashboard converts raw Superstore sales data into meaningful business insights using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Conditional Formatting**.

The main goal is to understand sales performance, identify loss-making transactions, and evaluate how discounts affect profitability.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall **Sales and Profit performance**
* Identify **loss-making transactions**
* Understand the impact of **Discount on Profit**
* Analyze **Category and Sub-Category performance**
* Explore **Regional, State, and City-level trends**
* Compare performance across different **Customer Segments**
* Analyze **Shipping Mode** performance
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## 📊 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

* 💰 **Total Sales:** 2.29M
* 💵 **Total Profit:** 286K
* 📉 Higher discounts are associated with reduced profitability.
* 🖥️ **Technology** is the most profitable category.
* 🪑 **Furniture** has comparatively lower profit margins.
* 🌆 Urban cities contribute significantly to overall sales.
* ⚠️ **1,871 transactions** are loss-making.

These findings highlight the importance of controlling discounts and monitoring profitability at the transaction and category levels.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the Excel project file.
2. Open the file using **Microsoft Excel**.
3. Navigate to the dashboard sheet.
4. Use the available **Slicers** to filter the data.
5. Analyze sales, profit, discounts, categories, regions, and customer segments.
6. Use the dashboard insights to understand business performance.

---

## 📌 Business Conclusion

The analysis shows that the business generates strong overall sales and profit, but **excessive discounts can negatively affect profitability**.

The dashboard can help businesses monitor product and regional performance, identify loss-making transactions, and make more informed pricing and discount decisions.
