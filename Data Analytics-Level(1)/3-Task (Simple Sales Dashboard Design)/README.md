# 📊 Simple Sales Dashboard Design

> A simple and interactive Power BI sales dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profit, customers, products, regions, and business trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Simple Sales Dashboard Design** is a Power BI data analytics project created to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales performance through **KPIs, charts, graphs, filters, and business insights**.

The main purpose of this project is to understand sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, regional performance, and profitability.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Track important business KPIs.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Analyze sales across different regions and states.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Compare sales and profit performance.
* Create an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.
* Generate useful business insights from sales data.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses a **SuperStore Sales Dataset** containing information related to orders, customers, products, sales, profit, quantity, discount, shipping, and geographical locations.

### Dataset Includes

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Ship Date
* Customer Details
* Product Details
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Sales
* Quantity
* Discount
* Profit
* Region
* State
* City
* Shipping Mode
